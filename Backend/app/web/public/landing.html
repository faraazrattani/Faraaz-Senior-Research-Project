What it does

MarketMind helps you analyze patterns around quarterly earnings: pre-earnings drift, gap reactions, and post-earnings momentum.

Run quick backtests, inspect signals, and view transparent explainability.

How it works

We combine market data with lightweight ML to produce Buy/Hold/Sell probabilities around earnings windows. You can tune windows, thresholds, and rules—then see an equity curve and metrics.
